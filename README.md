## Some unnecessary backgound

When I was a child I found a fascinating book in my grandparents' attic, where you could generate football league seasons by rolling dice to generate scores, filling in the results and final tables by pencil. Luckily my uncles had grown bored of this exercise halfway through, so I was able to finish the book (naturally I cheated in favour of Manchester United, my youthful favourite). Being decades old, the teams were not all the teams I was familiar with. This was perhaps my introduction to the strange historical depths of the intangible cultural heritage that is the English football pyramid. I suspect my enjoyment of this structure shares something with my enjoyment of novels with maps and appendices.
